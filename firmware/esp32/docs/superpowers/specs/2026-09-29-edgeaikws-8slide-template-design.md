## Scope and source distinction

The user explicitly selected an eight-slide working deck. The attached SIH2026 format PDF specifies a six-slide maximum and fixed official sections; therefore this eight-slide deck is a working presentation and is not represented as submission-compliant. No six-slide version is in scope.

Use `Dhvani_Kavach_SIH26052.pptx` only as a reference for composition and placement. Do not carry over its ANC-specific claims, project branding, team identifiers, measurements, or watermark. Preserve the previously created `EdgeAIKWS_SIH26172.pptx` and the original Dhvani deck.

## Design system

- Canvas: 20 × 11.25 inches (16:9), matching Dhvani.
- Visual language: restrained engineering/research presentation; white or very light backgrounds; navy/slate text; pale blue-gray panels; one muted orange accent; Arial typography.
- Layout: approximate Dhvani placements for the title, top corner identity marks, content panels, tables, image areas, and footer/page numbers.
- All headings, tables, diagrams, and image frames must be editable PowerPoint objects. Use labeled image frames, not invented screenshots or stock imagery.
- Include editable prompts such as `[Add ...]`; do not populate measured values, claims, references, dashboard content, or hardware photos.
- Replace Dhvani/team-specific branding with neutral `[Team logo]`, `[Project mark]`, and `[Insert image]` placeholders. A small SIH 2026 label may remain as editable text.

## Slide-by-slide layout

1. **TITLE PAGE** — Dhvani-style cover: SIH heading across the top, team identity placeholder at upper left, SIH identity area at upper right, metadata fields at left, large project-mark/image frame at right, project title/mark area along the lower left.
2. **IDEA TITLE** — Editable idea-title/proposed-solution panel across the upper area; a four-stage editable process row in the middle; an editable comparison/summary table across the lower area. These are blank labeled structures, not factual content.
3. **TECHNICAL APPROACH** — Large editable architecture/process diagram occupying the main left/center area, a narrow technology-stack panel at right, and a compact notes/callout area along the bottom.
4. **ML MODEL PARAMETERS & ESP32 DEPLOYMENT PARAMETERS** — Two aligned parameter tables/panels, ML model on the left and ESP32 deployment on the right. Labels guide editing; values remain placeholders.
5. **DASHBOARD IMAGES & HARDWARE IMAGES** — Large dashboard screenshot frame on the left; two hardware-image frames stacked on the right, each with an editable caption placeholder.
6. **FEASIBILITY & VIABILITY** — Editable existing-product comparison table across the upper portion; three lower panels for feasibility, viability, and challenges/mitigations.
7. **IMPACT & BENEFITS** — Six restrained editable benefit/impact cards in two rows, followed by a compact target-user/beneficiary row and an optional next-steps strip.
8. **RESEARCH & REFERENCES** — Large editable reference list on the left; a narrower foundational-work/source panel on the right; compact editable link/source placeholders along the bottom.
